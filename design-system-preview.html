+B FARMA Design System Analysis

A clean, high-trust healthcare and pharmacy design system anchored on a pure white canvas (#FFFFFF) with deep graphite ink (#32343D) and the vibrant brand voltage of +B Red (#E92125) that carries every primary CTA, search orb, and critical conversion moment. Typography runs the geometric family Lufga with soft rounded geometry (8px to 32px radii) across all surfaces.

Primary: #E92125 (+B Red)
Ink: #32343D
Hairline: #E7E7E7
Type: Lufga OTF Local
